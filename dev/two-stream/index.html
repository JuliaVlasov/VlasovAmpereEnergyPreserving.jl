Two-stream instability

Section 6.1 of the paper considers the one-dimensional Vlasov–Ampère system with the initial distribution

\[f_0(x,v) = \frac{v^2 e^{-v^2/2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \bigl(1+\alpha\cos(kx)\bigr).\]

The paper uses $k=0.5$, $\alpha=0.05$, $x\in[0,2\pi/k)$, $N_x=N_v=128$, $\Delta t=0.5$ and the final time $T=100$. It uses Fourier spatial discretization and degree-19 semi-Lagrangian interpolation ($d=9$). The paper compares VASAV, VA and Vlasov–Poisson schemes and studies Gauss-law and energy errors.

Run the Julia example

julia --project examples/two-stream-instability.jl

The paper does not specify a finite velocity interval; the example uses the periodic grid $v\in[-6,6)$. It runs only the SAV scheme, not the paper's VA and Vlasov–Poisson comparison or its time-step convergence study. The finite velocity interval and the even spatial grid contribute to nonzero discrete conservation errors. To inspect the returned histories:

include("examples/two-stream-instability.jl")
result = run_two_stream()
result.time
result.electric_energy
result.total_energy
result.poisson_error

total_energy uses the SAV energy; poisson_error is the discrete L2 residual of Gauss's law.
